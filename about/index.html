Our Story

Founded to raise the bar for audiology care in Bradenton

Coastal Hearing Care was founded to bring doctorate-level audiology to Bradenton and Lakewood Ranch, focused on education, best practices, and honest care rather than sales pressure.

Questions patients ask

About our audiology team

What does a Doctor of Audiology do?
A Doctor of Audiology (AuD) evaluates hearing, explains test results, fits hearing aids, and helps patients manage hearing concerns such as tinnitus. Coastal Hearing Care's providers hold Doctor of Audiology degrees.
How is a Doctor of Audiology different from a hearing aid specialist?
A Doctor of Audiology completes doctoral training, evaluates hearing and listening needs, explains results, and can help manage hearing concerns. A hearing aid specialist focuses mainly on selecting and fitting hearing devices within the scope of their license. Coastal Hearing Care's providers are Doctors of Audiology.
Who will care for me at Coastal Hearing Care?
Coastal Hearing Care's Doctors of Audiology are Dr. Kristin DesErmia, Dr. Danielle Marshall, and Dr. Arianna Davis. The team serves patients from offices in West Bradenton and Lakewood Ranch.
